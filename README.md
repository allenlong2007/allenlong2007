# Hi, I'm Allen

I'm studying Data Science at UC San Diego. Outside of class I build iOS apps, mostly for people I actually know, and one of them is live on the App Store.

## What I've built

**[Matching English and Chinese Sermons](https://github.com/allenlong2007/bilingual-sermon-analysis)**
I transcribed 29 hours of my church's English and Chinese podcasts with Whisper, then tested whether multilingual embedding models could match each English episode to its Chinese version without translating. The best one got 34 of 35. Python, pandas, scikit-learn, sentence-transformers.

**[拾穗 SJCA](https://github.com/allenlong2007/ChurchApp)** ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/%E6%8B%BE%E7%A9%97-sjca/id6814304145))
An English and Chinese app for my church to stream and download sermons, hymns, and videos. I built and shipped it on my own, including getting it through App Store review. New content goes live without an app update, through a small Python tool I wrote that publishes to Firebase.

**[CalendarApp](https://github.com/allenlong2007/CalendarApp)**
My daily calendar on iPhone and Mac. It started as a website, and I rebuilt it natively so it could have widgets, Lock Screen info, and Siri. It uses Apple's EventKit for real events and SwiftData for everything else.
